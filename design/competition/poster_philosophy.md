# Living Ledger

A design philosophy for scientific posters that must persuade a jury from three metres away and survive scrutiny from thirty centimetres.

**Space and form.** The page is a ledger, not a collage. A strict twelve-column grid with generous outer margins governs every edge; content aligns to it with surgical consistency, and nothing floats. Sections are separated by hairline rules and numbered in a monospaced index (01–06), the way a field notebook marks its entries. Boxes, shadows and rounded "cards" are almost entirely abandoned. Structure is carried by alignment, rules and white space, which only reads as calm when every gap has been measured, meticulously, until it disappears.

**Color and material.** Two materials only: warm paper and deep forest. The forest green arrives as large, confident fields (the title band and the method panel) that anchor the composition. Saffron is the single accent, reserved for the few numbers and lines that carry the argument, so the eye learns that saffron means "this matters". Sage is a quiet supporting tint for charts and diagram fills. Every other color is withheld. The palette should feel calibrated over many sittings, the product of painstaking restraint rather than decoration.

**Scale and rhythm.** Hierarchy is built from contrast of scale, not of color. A few numerals are monumental; labels are small, tracked-out monospace; prose is short and set in one comfortable size. Images are given room to be large and uninterrupted: the machine, its x-ray and the scenes of use are the evidence, so they occupy the space that text would otherwise claim. The rhythm alternates between dense, data-rich bands and open, breathing ones.

**Typography.** A literary serif speaks the ideas (titles, the hypothesis and the conclusion). A neutral grotesque carries explanation. A mono carries measurement: units, indices, sources. The three never trade roles. Line lengths are short, ragged edges are tuned by hand, and no paragraph exceeds a few lines. Master-level execution here means the type feels inevitable.

**Composition and balance.** Reading flows in a clear Z: from problem (left) to method (the heavy green field, right), down to evidence (left) and verdict (right), then across a full-width strip of use and recommendation. The weight of the dark method panel is countered by the large rendered machine in the conclusion, so the poster balances on its diagonal. The final work must look labored over by someone at the top of their field: every alignment checked, every caption tuned, every number sourced.

Research questions
  1. Which volume, OLR and HRT keep household food-waste digestion stable?
  2. How much biogas, cooking time and CO₂e can one unit deliver?
  3. Can sensor feedback make it usable for non-experts?
Research gap
  1. Home digesters are bulky, manure-fed and unmonitored.
  2. Food waste acidifies fast: VFA build-up halts methane.
  3. Cheap MQ sensors read ppm leaks, not biogas CH₄ %.
Hydrolysis→ monomers
Acidogenesis→ VFA, H₂
Acetogenesis→ acetate
Methanogenesis→ CH₄ + CO₂
Hypothesis

A shredded, stirred 100 L digester at OLR ≤ 3.4 reaches ≥ 50 % CH₄, and sensor guidance keeps usability at SUS ≥ 68.
